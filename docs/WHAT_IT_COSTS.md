# What it costs

Nothing.

Not a trial, not a free tier with the useful half behind a price, not thirty days.
Reading a history back off a phone that died, searching it, writing it out as web pages
you can keep, moving it onto another phone: all of it, free, with no account and no key.

That is a decision rather than a stage on the way to charging, and the reasoning is in
[ADR 10](adr/0010-a-tip-jar.md). The short version: this exists because somebody's
phone died and the official tools failed them three times, and asking that person for a
licence key at the moment they get their conversations back is not a business model.

## If it helped

There is one line in the whole program about money. It appears when an archive has been
written out, and when a migration has produced the backup — the two moments when
something that mattered has just worked — and it says you can buy the author a coffee
if you would like to.

**Suggested ten euros**, with five and twenty-five also on the page.

It is voluntary in the way that word is supposed to mean: nothing is withheld, nothing
is counted, nothing is remembered, and the program never mentions it again. It is not
shown when something has failed, which would be asking somebody to pay for a
disappointment.

## What this means for you

- **No account.** Nothing to sign up for, nothing to lose, no password.
- **No network.** The program makes no calls of any kind, and a page it never fetches
  cannot be a page that tracks you. The coffee page is a link you choose to open in
  your own browser.
- **No expiry.** The build you have keeps working, forever, whatever happens to this
  project.
- **No data, ever.** There is nothing to sell and nothing is collected. The whole design
  is arranged so that it cannot be.

## The engine is free software

AGPL-3.0, and that is not a marketing position: anybody asked to trust a program with
their entire correspondence should be able to read what it does with it, and anybody
who improves it has to publish that too. Build it yourself and you have exactly what is
in the download, minus the notarisation.

## What money would be spent on

If any arrives, in order: signing certificates, so that both operating systems stop
refusing the download and so that nobody can pass off a modified copy as this one; a
spare phone to prove the restore; and the domain.
